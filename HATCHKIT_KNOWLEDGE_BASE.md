## What Is HatchKit?

HatchKit is the all-in-one business platform built specifically for reptile breeders and vendors. It handles every part of running and growing a reptile business — from capturing leads at shows, to following up with buyers automatically, to checking weather before you ship a live animal — all from one place, all without you having to think about it.

The tagline: **"Your business, hatched and ready to grow."**

---

## Who Is It For?

HatchKit is built for reptile vendors and breeders who:

- Sell at reptile expos and shows (4–12 per year)
- Sell online through MorphMarket, Instagram, Facebook, or their own website
- Have 50–500+ animals in their collection
- Make $20,000–$150,000+ per year from reptile sales
- Are overwhelmed by the business side and just want to focus on their animals
- Are NOT tech-savvy — they use their phone for everything

**The core frustration HatchKit solves:**
> "I meet 200 people at a show and sell to maybe 10. The other 190 walk away and I have no way to reach them."

These are people who love their animals. They're great at breeding. They're not marketers, not web developers, not customer service teams. HatchKit does all of that for them.

## The Problem (Before HatchKit)

Here is what a typical reptile vendor's business looks like today:

### At the Show
- They set up a table with their animals
- People walk up, look, admire, ask questions
- Some buy. Most don't.
- The ones who don't buy just... walk away
- The vendor has no way to follow up with them
- By the end of the show, hundreds of potential buyers are gone forever

### After the Show
- DMs come in on Instagram
- Messages come in on MorphMarket
- Texts come in on their personal phone
- Facebook messages come in separately
- They're managing 4–5 different apps just to answer basic questions
- They inevitably miss messages, forget to follow up, and lose sales

### When It's Time to Ship
- They have to check the weather manually
- They're searching "weather in Phoenix this week" or "is it safe to ship to Dallas"
- They're guessing at whether to include a heat pack or cold pack
- If there's a weather hold, they have to call or text the buyer themselves to explain
- This is stressful, time-consuming, and easy to mess up

### Marketing
- They post on Instagram when they remember to
- They have no email list, or if they do, they never email it
- They don't have a real website — maybe a link tree or an old squarespace
- They have no way to tell past buyers when new animals are available

### The Result
- Great breeder. Struggling business.
- They're leaving money on the table at every single show.
- They're burning out managing communication manually.
- Buyers who would have purchased never hear from them again.

## The Solution: What HatchKit Does

HatchKit gives every breeder the same tools that major e-commerce brands use — fully configured, fully automated, fully built for live animal sales. Here's everything included:

---

### 1. Show Lead Capture
**The problem it solves:** People walk away from your booth and you never hear from them again.

**How it works:**
- HatchKit gives you a custom QR code to display at your table
- A buyer walks up, looks at your animals, scans the code with their phone
- A quick form pops up — they enter their name, email, and phone number
- Before they've walked three steps away, they've automatically received a thank-you text from you
- That person is now in your system forever
- They automatically get follow-up messages on Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, and Day 14 after the show — featuring your available animals, prices, and educational content
- You never send a single one of these manually

---

### 2. One Unified Inbox
**The problem it solves:** Managing DMs across 5 different apps and still dropping leads.

**How it works:**
- Every message from every platform comes into one place
- MorphMarket inquiries, Instagram DMs, text messages, email — all in a single inbox
- You never miss a message because you forgot to check one app
- AI drafts a reply for every message — you read it, tap approve, and it's sent
- You can also see your entire conversation history with any buyer in one thread

---

### 3. Automated Follow-Up Sequences
**The problem it solves:** Losing buyers who were interested but not ready to commit at the show.

**How it works:**
- Every lead from every show goes into an automated follow-up sequence
- Day 1: Thank-you text with your name and a link to your available animals
- Day 3: "Here's what we have available right now" with photos
- Day 7: Educational content about the species they were looking at
- Day 14: Special offer or call to action
- These run automatically for every lead, from every show, forever
- You configure it once. HatchKit runs it.

---

### 4. Smart Shipping (AI Shipping Agent)
**The problem it solves:** The stress and guesswork of shipping live animals safely.

**How it works:**
- When you're ready to ship an animal, HatchKit checks the weather at your location, the buyer's location, and every FedEx/UPS hub the package will pass through
- It knows the temperature tolerances for each species (leopard geckos, ball pythons, crested geckos, etc.)
- It automatically decides: safe to ship or hold, heat pack or cold pack, ship to address or hold at FedEx facility
- If it's not safe to ship, the buyer automatically gets a text explaining the delay — professionally worded, sent from your brand
- When the animal ships, the buyer gets a tracking notification automatically
- When it's delivered, a follow-up message goes out automatically asking how the animal arrived
- You never have to manually check a forecast again

---

### 5. Your Own Breeder Website
**The problem it solves:** No professional online presence; buyers can't find you outside of shows.

**How it works:**
- HatchKit builds you a real website with your brand, your logo, and your animals
- It includes a gallery of your available animals with photos and prices
- Buyers can submit inquiries directly from the website
- The site has care guides written for SEO so people searching for your species can find you
- It has an "About the Breeder" page so buyers know who they're buying from
- HatchKit maintains it — you don't have to touch it

---

### 6. Social Media on Autopilot
**The problem it solves:** Never posting consistently because there's no time or you don't know what to say.

**How it works:**
- HatchKit generates social media posts for your brand automatically
- Posts go out on a schedule to Instagram and Facebook
- The AI creates captions based on your animals, your morphs, your breeding projects
- When someone comments on your post asking about an animal, HatchKit can automatically capture that as a lead
- You can approve posts before they go out, or let them run fully automatically
- You stay visible and active on social without spending your evenings writing captions

---

### 7. Email Marketing
**The problem it solves:** Having a list of past buyers and interested people you never contact.

**How it works:**
- HatchKit maintains your email list automatically — everyone who ever scanned your QR, filled out a form, or bought from you is on it
- Automated campaigns go out for: new clutch announcements, seasonal availability, holiday promotions, show announcements
- You don't write any of it — HatchKit drafts the emails based on your inventory
- Past buyers who are ready for another animal hear from you before they go looking elsewhere

---

### 8. Sales Pipeline & Deal Tracking
**The problem it solves:** Losing track of who's interested in what, who's paid, who's waiting.

**How it works:**
- Every inquiry becomes a deal in your pipeline: Lead → Interested → Selected → Paid → Shipping → Delivered
- You can see exactly where every sale stands
- Automated payment reminders go out if someone hasn't paid their deposit
- Buyers who selected an animal can be added to a waitlist if something is sold out
- When a new clutch hatches, everyone on the waitlist for that species gets notified automatically

---

### 9. Animal Profiles
**The problem it solves:** No organized record of your animals, their history, or which buyer bought what.

**How it works:**
- Every animal in your collection has a profile: photos, morph, hatch date, feeding history, breeding notes
- When an animal sells, the buyer's info is attached to that animal's record forever
- If a buyer contacts you about an animal they bought two years ago, you can pull up the entire history instantly
- The system remembers which buyers are interested in which species, so you can notify the right people when the right animal is available
