🏠 Enclosure Setup

  • Size: 20-gallon long minimum for an adult (36"×18" preferred)
  • Substrate: Tile, paper towels, or reptile carpet — no loose substrate for juveniles
  • Hides: One on the warm side, one on the cool side, one humid hide (moist paper towels or sphagnum moss)
  • Water dish: Shallow dish, always fresh, changed every 2–3 days

🌡️ Temperature

  • Warm side: 88–92°F (belly heat for digestion)
  • Cool side: 72–78°F
  • Heat source: Under-tank heater (UTH) with a thermostat — not a heat lamp alone
  • Thermostat: Non-negotiable. A UTH without a thermostat can overheat and kill your Leopard Leopard Gecko.
  • Night temps: Can drop to 65°F — no problem

🍽️ Feeding

  • Staple feeders: Dubia roaches or crickets
  • Juveniles: Feed every day or every other day
  • Adults: Feed every 2–3 days
  • Gut-loading: Feed your insects a nutritious diet 24 hours before offering
  • Dusting: Calcium with D3 every other feeding; multivitamin once a week
  • Size rule: No feeder larger than the space between the Leopard Leopard Gecko's eyes

👋 Handling

  • First week: Hands off — let your Leopard Leopard Gecko decompress and settle in
  • After first week: Start with 5-minute sessions, build up gradually
  • Never grab from above — approach from the side, scoop from below
  • Tail drop: Leopard Leopard Leopard Leopard Geckos can drop their tails as a defense — it grows back, but avoid stressing them
  • Best time: Evening, when they're naturally more active

✅ New Leopard Gecko Checklist

☐  Enclosure fully set up before Leopard Leopard Gecko arrives
☐  Under-tank heater + thermostat installed and at temp
☐  3 hides in place (warm, cool, humid)
☐  Fresh water dish filled
☐  Feeders purchased and gut-loaded
☐  Calcium + multivitamin on hand
☐  Vet located (exotics/reptile vet, not a dog/cat clinic)
