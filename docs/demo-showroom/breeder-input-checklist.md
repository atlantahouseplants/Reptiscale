# Breeder Friend Input Checklist

Last updated: 2026-06-02

Use this when asking a real crested gecko breeder what would make the demo feel real.

## Highest Value Questions

1. What are the most common questions buyers ask before reserving a crested gecko?
2. What makes a buyer feel serious versus just browsing?
3. What deposit amount and deposit policy do you use or prefer?
4. What does your live-arrival guarantee say?
5. What shipping temperatures or conditions make you hold an animal?
6. What do you tell buyers to prepare before arrival?
7. What follow-up do you wish happened automatically after purchase?
8. What would you want to see first when logging into HighLevel every morning?
9. Which buyer groups do you want smart lists for?
10. What social posts are hardest to keep up with?

## Storefront Inputs

- Business name
- Owner name
- Species sold
- Show/event names
- Deposit policy
- Shipping policy
- Live-arrival guarantee language
- Pickup options
- Preferred contact method
- Brand colors
- Logo or logo preference

## Animal Listing Inputs

For each demo animal:

- name
- species
- morph
- sex
- weight
- price
- status
- temperament
- buyer fit
- feeding notes
- shipping notes
- photos or permission to use placeholders

## Buyer Journey Inputs

Ask what should happen when:

- someone downloads a care guide
- someone asks about a specific animal
- someone clicks reserve but does not pay
- someone pays a deposit
- weather is not safe to ship
- the animal ships
- the animal arrives
- the buyer confirms live arrival
- the buyer leaves a review
- the buyer refers a friend

## Content Inputs

- 10 common care tips
- 10 buyer objections
- 10 availability post ideas
- 5 behind-the-scenes post ideas
- 5 review/referral prompts
- tone examples from real posts or messages

## What We Can Fake For Demo V1

- animal photos, if needed
- logo
- demo business name
- demo policy wording
- demo emails/SMS
- demo payment/order flow
- demo social posts
- dummy contacts and conversations

## What We Should Not Fake For A Real Client Launch

- shipping origin address
- legal policy wording
- connected phone/email/payment settings
- live inventory
- real animal photos
- real prices
- real care instructions if the breeder disagrees with defaults
