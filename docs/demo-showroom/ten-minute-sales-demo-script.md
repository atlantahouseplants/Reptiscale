# 10-Minute SunScale Sales Demo Script

Last updated: 2026-06-02

## Demo Goal

Show a reptile breeder that Reptiscale is not just a website or CRM. It is the full buyer journey built in HighLevel:

capture -> follow-up -> animal interest -> reservation -> shipping review -> care -> review/referral -> repeat buyer.

## Setup Before The Call

Open these tabs:

1. SunScale storefront
2. Starter guide page
3. Mango animal detail page
4. Reservation page
5. HighLevel contact list
6. HighLevel workflows
7. HighLevel opportunities/pipelines
8. HighLevel smart lists
9. Shipping review/operator proof screen

Use a fresh demo buyer:

- Name: Demo Buyer
- Email: demo.lead@example.com or a fresh alias
- Phone: demo-safe phone number only
- Species: Crested Gecko
- Animal: Mango - Harlequin Dalmatian

Point out:

- storefront
- available animals
- starter guide
- reservation path

## What Not To Say

Avoid:

- `This is custom software we built from scratch.`
- `This automatically creates shipping labels.`
- `This guarantees sales.`
- `You will never need to touch HighLevel.`

Say instead:

- `This is a done-for-you HighLevel system.`
- `Shipping is review-only until a human approves it.`
- `This creates the buyer journey and follow-up system.`
- `You can manage everything from your HighLevel subaccount.`

## If The Prospect Asks What HatchKit Actually Does

Answer:

`HatchKit sets up and runs the business system around your reptile sales: your storefront, lead capture, buyer follow-up, CRM, inbox, reservation funnel, safe shipping review, care onboarding, reviews, referrals, and repeat-buyer campaigns. It is your branded HighLevel account, customized for your animals and your policies.`
