# Reptiscale Sales Playbook

## Sales Goal

Sell a working growth system, not software access.

The buyer should leave the demo believing:

- Their current lead follow-up is leaking money.
- Reptiscale fixes a real operational problem.
- They do not have to build it themselves.
- The system can pay for itself with a few recovered sales.

## Ideal Prospect

Good fit:

- Sells reptiles online, at shows, or through social media
- Has at least occasional paid orders already
- Has inconsistent follow-up
- Uses Instagram/Facebook/MorphMarket/DMs
- Has enough margin for setup plus monthly fee
- Wants to grow but is overwhelmed by business tasks

Poor fit:

- Pure hobbyist with no sales intent
- No inventory or upcoming clutches
- Refuses to use a CRM/inbox
- Wants a custom software build before paying
- Only wants free advice

## Discovery Questions

1. Where do most buyers first find you right now?
2. What happens when someone asks about an animal but does not buy that day?
3. How do you remember who asked about which animal?
4. How many show leads or DM leads do you think fall through each month?
5. Do you collect deposits, full payment, or both?
6. What part of shipping creates the most stress?
7. How often do you ask for reviews, referrals, or repeat purchases?
8. If this system recovered one or two missed sales per month, what would that be worth?

## Demo Flow

1. Start with the storefront.
2. Submit the starter guide form.
3. Show the contact created in HighLevel with tags and journey stage.
4. Show the animal reservation offer.
5. Show purchase follow-up and safe shipping logic.
6. Show the order-to-shipping operator review package.
7. Show care onboarding templates.
8. Show review, referral, and VIP repeat-buyer campaigns.
9. Close with pricing and the 14-day launch promise.

## Objections

### "I already use MorphMarket."

MorphMarket is a listing channel. Reptiscale is what captures everyone who does not buy immediately, follows up with them, and turns them into future buyers.

### "I do not have time to learn new software."

That is why this is done-for-you. You mainly need to check the inbox, move buyers through a simple pipeline, and approve content when needed.

### "I am not big enough for this."

Starter is for smaller breeders. If you lose even a few warm leads from shows or DMs, the system can be worth it before you become bigger.

### "Can I just build this myself in HighLevel?"

Yes, if you have the time and know the workflows. Reptiscale gives you the reptile-specific system, copy, tags, shipping logic, care sequences, and support already packaged.

### "I do not ship many animals."

The system still handles lead capture, nurture, deposits, pickup coordination, reviews, referrals, and repeat buyers. Shipping automation is one module, not the whole product.
