# Reptiscale Revenue Model

The fastest path to money is a productized service:

- Setup fee covers implementation labor.
- Monthly fee covers HighLevel management, support, content, optimization, and margin.
- Add-ons capture custom requests without bloating the base package.

## Margin Discipline

Protect profitability by tracking:

- Setup hours per customer
- Monthly support time per customer
- HighLevel cost per sub-account
- SMS/email usage
- AI/API usage
- Custom requests outside package scope

## Add-On Strategy

The best add-ons are recurring or repeatable:

- Done-for-you content management
- Monthly availability campaign
- Drop launch campaign
- Additional species setup
- Extra funnel or landing page
- Show campaign pack

## Early Customer Rule

Do not build custom one-off features for free. If a customer asks for something outside the package, turn it into:

- a paid add-on,
- a future roadmap item,
- or a manual workaround for the current month only.
