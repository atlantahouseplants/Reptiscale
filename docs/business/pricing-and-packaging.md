# Pricing And Packaging

Use setup fees plus monthly recurring revenue. This protects cash flow because early customers will need real implementation work.

## Recommended Packages

| Package | Setup | Monthly | Best For |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Starter | $799 | $199/mo | Small breeders who need lead capture, follow-up, and a simple online presence |
| Growth | $1,499 | $349/mo | Active sellers who need the full buyer journey, shipping workflow, and content engine |
| Scale | $2,499 | $599/mo | Established breeders with multiple species, regular drops, and higher support needs |

Growth should be the default recommendation.

## Starter

Includes:

- HighLevel sub-account setup
- Lead magnet and QR lead capture funnel
- Starter HighLevel Store/Website template
- CRM fields, tags, and smart lists
- New lead nurture workflow
- Review and referral workflow
- Monthly check-in

Limits:

- 1 species
- 8 social posts/month
- 3 active campaigns

## Growth

Includes Starter plus:

- Product detail, reservation, cart, checkout, and thank-you setup
- Product collections for available animals and supplies
- Post-purchase care onboarding
- Safe shipping decision workflow
- Availability alert campaign
- Social content calendar and approval flow
- Monthly performance review

Limits:

- Up to 3 species
- 20 social posts/month
- 7 active campaigns

## Scale

Includes Growth plus:

- Multi-species campaigns
- Drop launch campaign planning
- Advanced segmentation
- Referral/VIP program buildout
- Two monthly performance reviews
- Priority support

Limits:

- Up to 8 species
- 40 social posts/month
- 12 active campaigns

## Add-Ons

| Add-On | Price |
|---|---:|
| Additional species setup | $250 one-time |
| Launch content sprint | $500 one-time |
| Done-for-you monthly content management | $300/mo |
| Extra HighLevel workflow build | $150 one-time |

## Sales Rule

Do not discount monthly fees early. If a first customer needs a concession, discount setup or extend the onboarding window. Monthly revenue is what makes the business valuable.

## Guarantee

Suggested guarantee:

"If Hatchkit does not capture leads, follow up with them, and produce a working buyer journey within 14 days after you provide required access and assets, we keep working at no additional setup cost until it is live."

Avoid guaranteeing sales volume until there is enough data.
