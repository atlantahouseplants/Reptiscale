# Breeder User Guide

This is the simple guide to give a customer after Reptiscale is launched.

## What Reptiscale Does For You

Reptiscale helps move buyers through the whole journey:

1. They find your brand.
2. They join your list or ask about an animal.
3. They get follow-up and care education.
4. They view available animals.
5. They reserve or buy.
6. They get pickup, shipping, and care guidance.
7. They are asked for a review or referral.
8. They can join your VIP list for future animals.

## What You Need To Do

Daily:

- Check your inbox.
- Reply to serious buyers.
- Move buyers forward when a sale happens.
- Tell Reptiscale when an animal is sold, reserved, or newly available.

Weekly:

- Review new leads.
- Approve or edit social posts.
- Update available animals.
- Check buyers waiting on shipping or pickup.

Monthly:

- Review leads, sales, and follow-up.
- Pick the next campaign: show, availability drop, care content, referral push, or VIP list.

## Key Fields You Will See

- Species Interest: what the buyer is looking for
- Animal Interest: the animal or morph they asked about
- Offer Name: the current offer they saw
- Purchase Status: no purchase, deposit paid, paid in full, refunded, or cancelled
- Shipping Status: not started, pending weather check, approved, in transit, delivered, or live arrival confirmed
- Customer Journey Stage: where they are in the buyer journey
- Next Best Action: what should happen next

## What To Do When A Buyer Asks About An Animal

1. Open the contact.
2. Confirm their species and animal interest.
3. Answer their question.
4. Send the animal detail or reservation link.
5. If they seem serious, move them to the right pipeline stage or tag them as a hot lead.

## What To Do When A Deposit Is Paid

1. Mark Purchase Status as Deposit Paid.
2. Confirm animal interest.
3. Confirm pickup or shipping preference.
4. If shipping is needed, make sure destination ZIP is saved.
5. Let the shipping workflow check the route before scheduling.

## What To Do When Weather Is Unsafe

Do not argue with the system. Live animal safety wins.

1. Keep Shipping Status as Pending Weather Check or Hold.
2. Tell the buyer the animal is safe and eating.
3. Check again when the weather window improves.
4. Avoid promising a ship date until the route is safe.

## What To Do After Delivery

1. Confirm safe arrival.
2. Ask the buyer to send a photo.
3. Let care onboarding run.
4. Ask for a review.
5. Invite them to the VIP list.
6. Ask whether they know another buyer who needs the starter guide.

## When To Ask For Help

Ask for help when:

- A form is not creating contacts.
- A buyer received the wrong message.
- A payment happened but the buyer did not move forward.
- A shipping decision looks wrong.
- You need a new species, show, or campaign added.
