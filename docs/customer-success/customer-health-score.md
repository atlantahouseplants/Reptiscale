# Customer Health Score

Use this score to know which customers are getting value and which ones need help.

## Score Categories

Total: 100 points.

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| Lead capture active | 15 |
| New leads in last 30 days | 15 |
| Buyer follow-up workflows active | 15 |
| Sales or deposits recorded | 20 |
| Reviews/referrals requested | 10 |
| Social content approved or posted | 10 |
| Breeder responds in inbox/pipeline | 10 |
| No open P0/P1 issues | 5 |

## Health Bands

| Score | Status | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 80-100 | Healthy | Ask for testimonial, referral, or add-on |
| 60-79 | Watch | Review usage and improve one weak area |
| 40-59 | At Risk | Schedule support call and simplify workflow |
| 0-39 | Critical | Immediate intervention; identify blocker |

## Monthly Review Agenda

1. Leads captured
2. Best lead sources
3. Sales/deposits influenced
4. Shipping holds and safe arrivals
5. Reviews/referrals
6. Social content posted
7. Next month campaign
8. One recommended improvement

## Value Proof

Every review call should connect the system to business value:

- "These leads would have been lost."
- "These buyers got follow-up without manual work."
- "These customers were moved into repeat-buyer or referral flows."
- "This shipping hold was communicated clearly."
