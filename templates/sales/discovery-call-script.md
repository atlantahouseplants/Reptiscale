# Reptiscale Discovery Call Script

## Opening

Thanks for taking the time. I want to understand how you currently find buyers, follow up with them, and turn them into repeat customers. If it looks like Reptiscale can help, I can show you the demo and recommend the right package.

## Questions

1. What species do you sell most?
2. Where do most new buyers come from?
3. How many leads or inquiries do you get in a normal month?
4. What happens when someone asks about an animal but does not buy right away?
5. Do you sell at shows?
6. Do you collect deposits?
7. What part of shipping or pickup takes the most time?
8. How often do you ask for reviews or referrals?
9. What would make this system worth paying for?

## Qualification

Good fit if:

- They already sell animals.
- They have leads or inquiries.
- They want more structure.
- They value follow-up.
- They can afford setup and monthly fee.

## Transition To Demo

Based on what you described, the most relevant part of Reptiscale is the customer journey. I will show you how a buyer gets captured, followed up with, offered an animal, moved into purchase, supported through shipping/care, and invited back.
