# Reptiscale Outreach Sequence

## Message 1: Direct

Hey {{first_name}}, I am building Reptiscale for reptile breeders who sell through shows, social, MorphMarket, and DMs.

The basic idea: it gives you a storefront, lead capture, CRM, follow-up, reservation offers, shipping/care messages, reviews, referrals, and repeat-buyer campaigns in one system.

Would it be useful if buyers who ask about animals were automatically followed up with instead of disappearing in DMs?

## Message 2: Pain Follow-Up

Quick question: when someone asks about an animal but does not buy that day, do you have a system for following up with them later?

That is the gap Reptiscale is built around.

## Message 3: Demo Offer

I have a working crested gecko demo account I can show you. It walks through a buyer finding the breeder, getting a starter guide, viewing animals, placing a deposit, getting shipping/care follow-up, and later being asked for reviews/referrals.

Want me to send the demo link or show you in a quick call?

## Message 4: Last Touch

No pressure. I am talking to breeders now because I want this built around real seller workflows, not generic software assumptions.

If you ever want a system that captures leads and follows up with buyers automatically, I would be happy to show you what I built.
