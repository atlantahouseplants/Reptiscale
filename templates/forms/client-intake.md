# Reptiscale Client Intake Form

Use these fields in HighLevel, Google Forms, Typeform, or a simple intake document.

## Business Details

- Business name
- Owner name
- Owner email
- Owner phone
- City, state, ZIP
- Timezone
- Website URL
- Instagram URL
- Facebook URL
- MorphMarket URL

## Brand

- Logo upload
- Brand colors
- Brand voice
- Words or phrases to avoid
- What makes your animals different?
- What should buyers feel after working with you?

## Inventory

- Species sold
- Morphs/projects
- Current available animals
- Animal photos
- SKU or animal names
- Sex, weight, hatch date, temperament, price
- Which animals are beginner friendly?
- Which animals are breeder/collector focused?

## Sales Process

- Do you collect deposits?
- Deposit amount
- Refund policy
- Payment methods
- Pickup options
- Shipping options
- Shipping origin address for fulfillment review
- Preferred shipping carrier
- Live arrival guarantee terms
- Do you offer care kits or supplies?

## Customer Journey

- Best lead magnet topic
- Common beginner questions
- Most common buyer objections
- Current follow-up process
- Past buyer/referral process
- Review link
- VIP/waitlist preference

## Shows

- Upcoming shows
- Show dates
- Show cities
- Show pickup offers
- QR lead capture needs

## Access

- HighLevel access
- Payment provider access
- Social account access
- Domain/DNS access if needed
